Changelog

What changed in each App Store release of PollenPath, newest first.

v1.0.0Coming soon
  • The first release on the App Store.
  • Free: log any of 25 common allergy symptoms in a couple of taps, or add your own. Symptoms from the built-in list are saved to Apple Health. After a week of logging, PollenPath shows the three weather conditions that track your symptoms most closely, worked out on your iPhone. There's also a weekly summary, a 30-day history calendar and tomorrow's weather outlook.
  • Basic adds live pollen levels for trees, grasses and weeds, current air quality with a health tip, tomorrow's pollen outlook, daily pollen alerts and 90 days of history. Live pollen data isn't available in every country.
  • Pro adds a 5-day pollen forecast, the plant species behind each reading, a year of history, your own pollen alert threshold, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and PDF and CSV export. After three weeks of logging, it also predicts how strong your symptoms will be each day.
  • No account, no ads and no tracking. Your symptom data stays in Apple Health and your own iCloud account.
