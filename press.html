About the app

Short (one line)

PollenPath: Allergy Tracker. Log allergy symptoms for free, keep them in Apple Health, and see which weather lines up with your worst days.

Medium (~100 words)

PollenPath is an allergy diary for iPhone. You log how you feel, the app saves it to Apple Health, and over time it shows which conditions line up with your bad days. Logging and the weather analysis are free, with no account and no ads.

Basic adds live pollen levels for trees, grasses and weeds, current air quality and daily pollen alerts. Pro adds a 5-day pollen forecast, the plant species behind each reading, widgets, PDF and CSV export, and daily symptom predictions from a model trained on your iPhone.

Long (~300 words)

PollenPath is an allergy diary for iPhone. You log how you feel, the app saves it to Apple Health, and over time it shows which conditions line up with your bad days. Logging and the weather analysis are free, with no account and no ads.

Free. Log any of 25 common allergy symptoms in a couple of taps, or add your own. Symptoms from the built-in list are saved to Apple Health. After a week of logging, PollenPath compares your symptoms with temperature, humidity, wind, chance of rain, dew point and pressure changes, then shows the three conditions that track your symptoms most closely. The analysis runs on your iPhone. A weekly summary shows how the last seven days went and which symptoms came up most. The history calendar covers your last 30 days, and the Forecast tab gives you a weather outlook for tomorrow.

Basic adds live pollen levels for trees, grasses and weeds, current air quality with a health tip, tomorrow’s pollen outlook, daily pollen alerts and 90 days of history. Live pollen data isn’t available in every country.

Pro adds a 5-day pollen forecast, the plant species behind each reading, a year of history, your own pollen alert threshold, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and PDF and CSV export. After three weeks of logging, Pro also predicts how strong your symptoms will be each day, using a model trained on your iPhone from your own history.

Privacy. There’s no account, no advertising and no tracking. PollenPath has no server of its own, so your data never reaches us. Symptoms from the built-in list stay in Apple Health. Custom symptoms, check-ins, settings, insights and saved pollen and weather readings sync between your devices through your own iCloud account. To show local conditions, the app sends your location to its weather, pollen and air quality providers.

PollenPath is a wellness and tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
