Support

Get help with PollenPath

Contact us

Email support@pollenpath.app. You can also tap Contact Support under About in the app's Settings.

If something isn't working, tell us what you did just before it happened, your iPhone model and iOS version, and the PollenPath version shown in Settings → About.

Subscriptions

  • New iPhone or reinstalled the app? Open Settings in PollenPath and tap Restore Purchases.
  • To change or cancel, tap Manage Subscription in PollenPath's Settings, or go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.
  • Refunds go through Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How PollenPath handles your data is in the Privacy Policy. The rules for using the app are in the Terms of Service.
