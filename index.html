Questions

Is PollenPath a medical app?

No. It's a wellness and tracking tool that helps you notice patterns between how you feel and what's in the air. It doesn't give medical advice or a diagnosis. For medical concerns, talk to a clinician.

Where does the data come from?

Pollen and air quality come from Google, and the weather from Apple Weather. Coverage varies by country, and some local plants may not be included.

How is my privacy protected?

There's no account and no PollenPath server. Your symptoms live in Apple Health and your own iCloud, and the trigger analysis runs on your iPhone. The app sends your location to fetch pollen, air quality and weather. No ads, no trackers.

What do I get for free?

Symptom logging with 25 built-in symptoms plus your own, trigger analysis of six weather conditions after a week, a weekly summary, 30 days of history and tomorrow's weather outlook. Basic and Pro add live pollen, air quality, forecasts and longer history.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 26.4 or later. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets come with Pro.
